## 🎨 Editing Checklist

### Pre-Recording
- [ ] Clear browser cache (no distracting tabs)
- [ ] Set screen resolution to 1080p
- [ ] Test microphone audio levels
- [ ] Prepare sample emails in the app
- [ ] Practice the click flow 2-3 times

### Recording
- [ ] Record in one continuous take if possible
- [ ] Speak clearly, moderate pace
- [ ] Pause briefly between scenes (easier to edit)
- [ ] Move mouse smoothly, not frantically

### Post-Production
- [ ] Trim dead space and long pauses
- [ ] Add cursor highlight (if not built-in)
- [ ] Insert transitions between scenes
- [ ] Add background music (low, 10-15% volume)
- [ ] Add text overlays for key points
- [ ] Color grade for consistency
- [ ] Add intro/outro graphics
- [ ] Export at 1080p, 30fps

### Audio
- [ ] Normalize audio levels
- [ ] Remove background noise
- [ ] Add subtle sound effects:
  - Alert sound for BLOCK
  - Success sound for ALLOW
  - Transition whooshes

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Keep it visual** - Show, don't tell. Let the app speak for itself.
2. **Be authentic** - Don't over-polish. Judges appreciate genuine demos.
3. **Highlight the AI** - Make sure Gemini 3's role is clear and prominent.
4. **End strong** - The closing should be memorable and reinforce your message.
5. **Test on mobile** - Some judges watch on phones. Ensure text is readable.
